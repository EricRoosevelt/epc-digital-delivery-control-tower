## What this research permits

Prepare a domain-reviewed checklist with links and original descriptions. It
does not permit importing third-party code, distributing source documents,
copying a proprietary ruleset or claiming a legally complete rule catalogue.
Public web guidance, open-source code and commercially licensed content are
different things. The repository's third-party intake rules still apply.

Before implementing a retained check, record its exact document edition and
section/worksheet/row, applicability, required model facts, interpretation,
reviewer and test counterexample. For an admitted downloaded asset, also record
licence/permission, version and SHA-256 under the repository intake process.
No hashes are invented for linked files we have not downloaded.

Do not hard-code dimensional fire-safety/accessibility limits from general
knowledge. Relevant primary provisions and exceptions have not been inspected
in this round. Those legal/code checks remain outside the first draft.

For a future commercial offering, the credible initial value is configurable
preflight and traceable remediation, not ownership of government requirements.
Customer conventions need their own provenance and project applicability;
authority sources must not be silently overridden by a customer preference.
